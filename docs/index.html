The war

  • Territory is the economy. Nothing to mine, nothing to collect. Every sector you hold cuts the time your factories need.
  • Nothing goes to waste. Tanks, jeeps, guns and cranes sit abandoned all over the map. Walk a robot up to one and he climbs in. Shoot the driver out of an enemy tank and the tank is yours.
  • Six robot classes. Grunts, psychos, snipers, toughs, pyros, lasers. They swear, they get bored, they call for help, and they scramble when a shell lands close.
  • Blow the bridge. Infantry fords a river. Armour does not.
  • Twenty missions, five planets, plus 62 skirmish maps. Desert, volcanic, arctic, jungle, city.
  • Take his fort or lose yours. That is the whole of it.
